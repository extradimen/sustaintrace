# SustainTrace manuscript claim boundaries v1.0

## Claims supported by the current evidence

- SustainTrace creates atomic ESG knowledge records linked to source pages, quotations and
  available table or layout coordinates.
- The 20-report human benchmark found exact evidence coordinates for all 40 candidate tasks.
- Applicable candidate slots were exact in 177 of 220 adjudicated cases.
- No unsupported or unassessable claim was admitted among the three frozen Tier B promotions.
- In the 20-report E3 paired pseudo-gold experiment, the deterministic admission gate reduced
  unsafe admission from 27.0% to 1.0% for Mistral Large 3 and from 24.2% to 1.0% for GPT-OSS on
  identical frozen candidates; no unsafe admission was introduced in either paired comparison.
- E3 safe-admission retention was 10/12 for Mistral and 7/13 for GPT-OSS, so the safety benefit and
  model-specific over-blocking cost are both supported and must be reported together.
- The system is conservative: false abstention and human-admissible held candidates are reported
  explicitly rather than hidden.
- Immutable unseen component evidence covers six repair-component classes and seven reports.
- Three component families preserve a failure-to-development-to-new-unseen-pass sequence.
- Frozen lifecycle controls detect hash, invariant and preflight faults and terminate in promotion,
  rollback or block without modifying source artifacts or historical lockbox results.

## Claims that must not appear

- SustainTrace verifies the real-world truth of issuer disclosures.
- SustainTrace extracts every fact accurately or handles arbitrary complex tables.
- The current Tier B admission precision is high or definitive; its denominator is only three.
- E3 establishes human-adjudicated semantic accuracy, external factual truth or fully unseen
  report generalization; its reference is source-derived deterministic-mutation pseudo-gold, and
  some source reports were present in the wider development corpus.
- The paired safety effect is cost-free or model invariant; GPT-OSS safe admission fell by 30
  percentage points.
- Tier C records are final rejections; they form a review queue.
- The system has demonstrated general autonomous repair or autonomous code self-modification.
- Any repair component has reached repeated heterogeneous unseen success (L4).
- The 13 component trials and four execution-control cases form one pooled repair-accuracy metric.
- P9 may contribute to a cross-version aggregate score; its frozen reference/window integrity was
  invalid for that purpose.

## Required wording distinctions

- Use **evidence fidelity** rather than factual truth.
- Use **controlled evolution** rather than unconstrained self-evolution.
- Use **human-gated trusted promotion** rather than automatic trusted knowledge creation.
- Report a **bounded correction** separately from an unsafe unsupported admission.
- Describe RQ3 as **heterogeneous component evidence plus lifecycle safety controls**, not broad
  repair-effectiveness validation.
- Describe E3 as a **paired deterministic admission-control effect on identical candidates**, not a
  second independent semantic-accuracy benchmark.
