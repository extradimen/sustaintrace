# Cover letter — SoftwareX

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We submit the manuscript **“SustainTrace: Evidence-grounded ESG knowledge infrastructure with
controlled evolution”** for consideration in *SoftwareX*.

SustainTrace is open research software for converting corporate ESG and annual reports into
atomic, evidence-linked facts while retaining typed extraction failures as reusable method
knowledge. Its distinguishing software contribution is an auditable knowledge-admission boundary:
claims remain linked to PDF pages and layout evidence, unsupported candidates are withheld, and
bounded repairs must pass deterministic post-validation and human-gated promotion.

The validation was designed to establish software utility rather than implementation correctness
alone. It includes (i) an independently adjudicated 60-task benchmark spanning 20 official reports,
(ii) a 120-task paired experiment for each of two language models in which ungated and full-system
decisions are derived from the same frozen candidate, and (iii) immutable heterogeneous component
and repair-lifecycle evidence. The results quantify both benefits and limitations: the admission
gate reduced unsafe admission to 1.0% for both models, while model-dependent reductions in safe
admission expose the current cost of conservative control. The manuscript explicitly limits its
claims to report fidelity, admission control and bounded repair evidence; it does not claim external
truth verification or general autonomous self-repair.

The software is publicly available under the Apache License 2.0:

- GitHub release: https://github.com/extradimen/sustaintrace/releases/tag/v0.1.1
- Archived release: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23293034

The archive includes the cross-platform software, documentation, evaluation artifacts and
manuscript source. Issuer PDFs are not redistributed because they remain under their publishers'
copyright.

The authors declare no competing interests and no external funding. Ethics approval was not
required because the evaluation involved professional annotation of public corporate reports and
software outputs, collected no sensitive personal data and involved no intervention with human
participants.

The manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere. All
authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to *SoftwareX*.
